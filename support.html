Why did today's workout change?

Mango adjusts your plan when something changes: a missed session, a low readiness reading in the morning, or a change to your events, weekly hours or long-session days. When today's session changes, Today shows a short note saying why. Profile explains how the whole plan is built.

How do workouts get to my Apple Watch?

With Mango Plus, the next seven days of workouts are sent to the Workout app on your watch, with your zones and targets. If you didn't allow it when asked, turn it on from the "Send to your Apple Watch" link on Today, or from Profile.

How do I get rides on MyWhoosh?

Connect intervals.icu in Mango's Profile, then link MyWhoosh in your intervals.icu settings. intervals.icu sends planned rides on to MyWhoosh, up to seven days ahead.

The readiness check or fitness chart is empty

Mango needs Apple Health access, and an Apple Watch or other device that records heart rate, HRV and sleep. Check Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Mango and turn the data types on.

How do I restore Mango Plus on a new iPhone?

Open the Mango Plus screen from Profile and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions and refunds are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, and request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of it. Rides already on your intervals.icu calendar stay there until you delete them in intervals.icu.
