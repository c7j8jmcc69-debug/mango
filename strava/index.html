Why riders use it

Indoor rides, sorted. Planned rides go to your free intervals.icu calendar, ready in MyWhoosh, and the rides you finish count toward your plan (Mango Plus).

A plan that peaks on race day. Base, build, peak and taper, worked back from your event and fitted to the hours you have.

Adapts every day. Each morning Mango Pro compares your resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and sleep with your usual, and suggests an easier day when you need one (Mango Plus).

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plan and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone.
