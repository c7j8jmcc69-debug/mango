Mango Pro

A half marathon plan that fits your week

Whether it's your first 13.1 or a new PB, Mango Pro builds the plan from your race date and the days you can train, and adjusts it as you go.

How long the plan takes

Eight weeks is the minimum, and 10 to 14 is comfortable for most runners.

How much time you need

Around 3 hours a week covers most half marathon plans. Tell Mango Pro what you have and it plans around it.

What a week looks like

A typical week: easy runs, one tempo or interval session, a long run that builds towards the distance, and optional strength on easy days.

Arriving fresh

The final 10 days ease off so you race fresh.

Why Mango Pro

Adapts every day. Each morning Mango Pro compares your resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and sleep with your usual. When you're off, it suggests an easier day and the week rebalances (Mango Plus).

On your wrist. Sessions go to the Workout app on your Apple Watch with your zones and targets (Mango Plus).

Measured, not guessed. Finish a workout and Mango Pro compares it with the plan: time, pace or power, time in zone and distance.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plan and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone.

Questions

How many weeks do I need for a half marathon?

At least 8. Mango Pro works back from your date and tells you if it's tight.

Can beginners use it?

Yes. Set your experience to beginner and the plan grows more slowly, with more easy running.

Does it work without an Apple Watch?

Yes. The plan, workout reviews and predictions work from your iPhone and Apple Health. Apple Watch workouts are part of Mango Plus.

Build my plan

Mango Pro gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new programme.
