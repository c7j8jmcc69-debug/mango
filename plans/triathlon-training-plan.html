Mango Pro

A triathlon plan for all three sports

Sprint, Olympic, half or full distance: Mango Pro balances swim, bike and run around your week, includes bricks, and keeps the plan in step with your recovery.

Download Mango Pro on the App Store
Free on iPhone. Mango Plus from £7.99 a month, or £49.99 a year with a one-month free trial.

How long the plan takes

At least 6 weeks for a sprint, 8 for an Olympic, 12 for a half (70.3) and 16 for a full distance.

How much time you need

Roughly 2.5 hours a week for a sprint, 4 for an Olympic, 6 for a half and 8 or more for a full. Mango Pro plans around the hours you give it.

What a week looks like

A typical build week mixes swims with sets at your CSS pace, rides (indoors on MyWhoosh if you like), runs including a brick off the bike, and optional strength.

Arriving fresh

A half-distance taper lasts two weeks and a full-distance one three, with short, sharp sessions to stay fresh.

Why Mango Pro

Adapts every day. Each morning Mango Pro compares your resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and sleep with your usual. When you're off, it suggests an easier day and the week rebalances (Mango Plus).

On your wrist. Sessions go to the Workout app on your Apple Watch with your zones and targets (Mango Plus).

Measured, not guessed. Finish a workout and Mango Pro compares it with the plan: time, pace or power, time in zone and distance.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plan and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone.

Questions

Does it plan brick sessions?

Yes. A brick shows as the ride and the run straight off the bike, on Today and in Week.

Can indoor rides go to MyWhoosh?

Yes, with Mango Plus: indoor rides go to your free intervals.icu calendar, which sends them on to MyWhoosh.

Is Mango Pro affiliated with IRONMAN?

No. It's an independent app; "Ironman" and "70.3" describe the race distances.

Build my plan

Mango Pro gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new programme.
