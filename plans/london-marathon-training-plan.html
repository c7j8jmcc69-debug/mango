Training for the London Marathon

How long the plan takes

Starting in December gives you around 18 weeks: time for a proper base through the winter, a build in February and March, and a three-week taper in April.

How much time you need

Plan for at least 4 hours a week. Mango Pro fits sessions to the days you can train and moves your long run to your long-run day.

What a week looks like

Winter weeks are mostly easy running with one quality session and a long run that grows slowly. Rain at your session time? With Mango Plus, you get a heads-up and a one-tap switch to the treadmill.

Arriving fresh

The last three weeks taper, so you reach the start line fresh. Training races, such as a spring half marathon, fit in with a few easier days around them.

Questions

When should I start training for the London Marathon?

About 16 to 20 weeks before, so December for an April race. Mango Pro needs at least 12 weeks.

Can I add a half marathon before London?

Yes. Add it as a training race and Mango Pro gives it a few easier days without breaking your marathon build.

Is Mango Pro an official London Marathon app?

No. Mango Pro is an independent training app and isn't affiliated with the London Marathon.

Build my plan
