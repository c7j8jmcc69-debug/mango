A cycling plan shaped by your event

Add your event's distance, climbing and whether it's road, gravel or mountain bike, and Mango Pro builds rides that prepare you for it, with power targets from your FTP.

How long the plan takes

Eight weeks is the minimum for a gran fondo or sportive, and six for a time trial.

How much time you need

About 4 hours a week for a gran fondo. Long rides grow towards the event's length without going past it.

What a week looks like

A typical week: endurance rides, a sweet-spot or threshold session, a long ride on your long day (with climbing for hilly events), and optional strength.

Arriving fresh

The last 10 days ease off so your legs are fresh for the climbs.

Why Mango Pro

Adapts every day. Each morning Mango Pro compares your resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and sleep with your usual. When you're off, it suggests an easier day and the week rebalances (Mango Plus).

On your wrist. Sessions go to the Workout app on your Apple Watch with your zones and targets (Mango Plus).

Measured, not guessed. Finish a workout and Mango Pro compares it with the plan: time, pace or power, time in zone and distance.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plan and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone.

Questions

Where does my FTP come from?

Enter your own, or Mango Pro works it out from your hard efforts in the last six weeks and suggests updates as your fitness changes.

Can I ride indoors?

Yes. Switch any ride to indoor; with Mango Plus it goes to MyWhoosh through intervals.icu, and finished rides come back and count.

Does it predict my finish time?

Yes, from your FTP, the distance, the climbing and the riding type.

Build my plan

Mango Pro gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new programme.
