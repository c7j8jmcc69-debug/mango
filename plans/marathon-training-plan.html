How long the plan takes

Give yourself at least 12 weeks, and 16 to 20 if you can. Mango Pro works back from race day and warns you if the time is tight.

How much time you need

Most marathon plans need at least 4 hours a week. Mango Pro plans around the hours you give it and grows your long run gradually.

What a week looks like

A typical build week has easy runs, one quality session (tempo or intervals), a long run on your long-run day that practises fuelling, and two short optional strength sessions.

Arriving fresh

Your last three weeks taper: volume comes down while some intensity stays, so you start fresh.

Why Mango Pro

Adapts every day. Each morning Mango Pro compares your resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and sleep with your usual. When you're off, it suggests an easier day and the week rebalances (Mango Plus).

Questions

How long is a marathon training plan?

Usually 16 to 20 weeks. Mango Pro needs at least 12 and builds base, build, peak and a three-week taper from your race date.

Can I train for a marathon in 12 weeks?

If you already run regularly, yes. Mango Pro starts from what you run now, using your Apple Health history, and says so if the goal is ambitious for the time left.

Will it predict my marathon time?

Yes. Mango Pro predicts your finish time from your threshold pace and your Apple Watch VO2 max, carried forward to the fitness you'll have on race day.

Build my plan

Mango Pro gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new programme.
