Mango Pro

A 10K plan pitched to your pace

Mango Pro builds a 10K plan from your date and goal, with interval and tempo targets from your own threshold pace, which it can work out from your recent runs.

How long the plan takes

Six weeks is the minimum, and 8 to 10 gives you room to build.

How much time you need

Two hours a week is enough to train well for a 10K.

What a week looks like

A typical week: two easy runs, one interval or tempo session and a longer easy run.

Arriving fresh

The last week eases off, with a short opener the day before.

Why Mango Pro

Adapts every day. Each morning Mango Pro compares your resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and sleep with your usual. When you're off, it suggests an easier day and the week rebalances (Mango Plus).

On your wrist. Sessions go to the Workout app on your Apple Watch with your zones and targets (Mango Plus).

Measured, not guessed. Finish a workout and Mango Pro compares it with the plan: time, pace or power, time in zone and distance.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plan and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone.

Questions

Can I set a custom distance, like a 7K?

Yes. Pick Custom and the plan scales to your distance.

Where do my paces come from?

From your threshold pace. Enter your own, or Mango Pro works it out from the hard efforts in your last six weeks of runs.

Does it predict my 10K time?

Yes, from your threshold pace and Apple Watch VO2 max, with a range.

Build my plan

Mango Pro gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new programme.
