Built around your race

A season that peaks on race day.

Base, build, peak and taper, worked back from your goal race. Training builds a little each week, with easier weeks to absorb it, so you arrive fresh.

  • Pitched for you. Your experience, your hours and your training days set the volume, the hard sessions and the long ones.
  • More than one race. Main goals get a full peak and taper. Training races get a few easier days. Fun runs fit around the plan.
  • Your course, your plan. Add distance, climbing and road, gravel or mountain bike, and the rides train for it.

Listens to your body

Train hard on the right days.

Each morning Mango Pro reads your resting heart rate, HRV and sleep from Apple Health and compares them with your usual. When they're off, it suggests an easier day, and the rest of your week adjusts if you take it.

  • Weather-aware. Rain at your session time? Take it indoors, or go later.
  • Life happens. Feeling tired or missed a session? The rest of the week rebalances around it.
