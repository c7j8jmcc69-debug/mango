Mango

Your race. Your plan. Let's go.

Mango builds a training plan for your goal race, for running, cycling, swimming or triathlon, and keeps it in step with you. Each morning it checks your resting heart rate, HRV and sleep from Apple Health, and the weather for your session, and adjusts the day's workout when it needs to.

Built around your race. Base, build, peak and taper, timed so you arrive fresh on race day. The load builds gradually, with an easier week every fourth.

On your wrist and your trainer. Workouts go to the Workout app on your Apple Watch, and indoor rides can go to MyWhoosh through your intervals.icu calendar.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plan and health data stay on your iPhone.
