Mango

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Mango ("the app") is made by Christian Augustuss Bergara ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version: we don't collect any of your data. Your plan, your health data and your settings stay on your iPhone. The only time anything leaves it is when you choose to connect intervals.icu, and then only your planned rides go to your own intervals.icu calendar.

What the app stores

The app stores your goal events, your training plan, the details you enter about your training (experience, weekly hours, training days and thresholds such as pace, FTP and heart rate) and your settings. All of it is kept only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we can't see your data.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads your workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV) and sleep from Apple Health. It uses them on your iPhone to check whether you're ready to train, to match finished workouts to your plan and to chart your fitness. It saves the workouts you schedule so they appear on your Apple Watch. Health data is never sent to us or to anyone else, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with intervals.icu. You can change what the app can read in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Mango.

Apple Watch

With your permission, the app schedules your upcoming workouts in the Workout app on your Apple Watch, using Apple's WorkoutKit. This happens between your iPhone and your watch.

Weather and location

With your permission, the app uses your approximate location to get the forecast for your sessions from Apple Weather. Your location is used only for that, is not stored by us, and is not used to track you. You can turn location off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Mango.

intervals.icu (optional)

If you choose to connect your intervals.icu account, the app adds your planned rides (name, date, description and workout steps) to your own intervals.icu calendar, so intervals.icu can send them on to MyWhoosh or other trainer apps you've linked there. It also checks whether MyWhoosh is linked. Your access key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. The app doesn't read your activities or wellness data from intervals.icu. intervals.icu's own privacy policy applies to data held in your intervals.icu account. You can disconnect at any time in the app's Profile, or revoke access in intervals.icu's settings.

Apple Intelligence

When Apple Intelligence is available, the app uses Apple's on-device model to tailor your upcoming weeks. This runs on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us or to a cloud service.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, or any third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

The optional Mango Pro upgrade is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from Profile, the app opens an email for you to read, change and send yourself. It contains only what you type, plus the app and iOS version. Your health data and plan are never included. We use feedback emails only to reply to you and to improve the app, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

Deleting your data

You can delete events and change your details in the app. Disconnecting intervals.icu removes its key from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Workouts already added to your intervals.icu calendar stay there until you delete them in intervals.icu.

Health and safety

Mango's plans are general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new programme, and stop if you feel unwell.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
