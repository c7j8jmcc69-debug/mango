Mango Pro

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Mango ("the app") is made by Christian Augustuss Bergara ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version: we collect almost nothing. Your plan, your health data and your settings stay on your iPhone. A few things leave it, all only when you choose: if you connect intervals.icu, your planned rides go to your own intervals.icu calendar and the app reads back the activities you finish there; if you connect Strava, the app finds the activities that match your planned sessions and adds a short note to them; and if you send feedback from the app, we receive what you write, plus a few details that help us fix problems.

What the app stores

The app stores your goal events, your training plan, the details you enter about your training (experience, weekly hours, training days and thresholds such as pace, FTP and heart rate, whether you entered them or the app worked them out from your workouts) and your settings. All of it is kept only on your device. We have no accounts, and we can't see your plan or health data. Our only server receives feedback you choose to send (see Feedback).

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads your workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), breathing rate, VO2 max, sleep, workout distance (walking and running, cycling and swimming) and cycling power from Apple Health. It uses them on your iPhone to check whether you're ready to train, to predict your race times, to match finished workouts to your plan, to compare each workout with what was planned (time, pace or power, time in zone and distance), to chart your fitness, and to work out your thresholds (threshold pace, FTP, swim pace and threshold heart rate) from the hard efforts in your last six weeks. It saves the workouts you schedule so they appear on your Apple Watch. If you connect intervals.icu, it also saves the activities you finish there (for example, MyWhoosh rides) to Apple Health when Health doesn't already have them, with their distance, heart rate, power and an estimate of energy burned, so they appear in Health and the Fitness app. Health data is never sent to us or to anyone else, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with intervals.icu. You can change what the app can read in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Mango.

Apple Watch

With your permission, the app schedules your upcoming workouts in the Workout app on your Apple Watch, using Apple's WorkoutKit. This happens between your iPhone and your watch.

Weather and location

With your permission, the app uses your approximate location to get the forecast for your sessions from Apple Weather. Your location is used only for that, is not stored by us, and is not used to track you. You can turn location off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Mango.

intervals.icu (optional)

If you choose to connect your intervals.icu account, the app adds your planned rides (name, date, description and workout steps) to your own intervals.icu calendar, so intervals.icu can send them on to MyWhoosh or other trainer apps you've linked there. It also checks whether MyWhoosh is linked.

It also reads the activities you finish (for example, rides recorded in MyWhoosh): the sport, start time, duration, distance, average heart rate and power, and minute-by-minute power, heart rate and distance. It uses them on your iPhone to mark sessions done, show how they compared with the plan, chart your fitness and work out your thresholds, exactly as it does with Apple Health workouts, and saves them to Apple Health as described above. Nothing it reads from intervals.icu is sent to us or anyone else. It doesn't read your wellness data.

Your sign-in is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. intervals.icu's own privacy policy applies to data in your intervals.icu account. You can disconnect at any time in the app's Profile, or revoke access in intervals.icu's settings.

Strava (optional)

If you connect Strava, Mango reads your recent Strava activities to find the ones that match your planned sessions, and adds a short note to them. Nothing else is read or kept, and you can disconnect at any time.

The note says what the session was, how much it added to your fitness and why it mattered, with a link to Mango. It never includes your races, goals, target times or health data. Notes are off until you turn them on, and you can also choose to rename the activity to the session's name. Mango never uploads, deletes or otherwise changes your activities.

When you connect, Strava's sign-in code passes through our small sign-in service, which swaps it for access and passes that straight back to your iPhone without keeping anything. Your access is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. Strava data is never used with any AI. Strava's own privacy policy applies to data in your Strava account. Disconnect in the app's Profile, or in Strava's settings.

Apple Intelligence

When Apple Intelligence is available, the app uses Apple's on-device model to tailor your upcoming weeks. This runs on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us or to a cloud service.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share personal data, and we collect none apart from feedback you choose to send.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, or any third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

The optional Mango Plus upgrade is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Profile, the app sends us:

  • what you write
  • your answers to "How did it go?" and "Which part of Mango?"
  • your email address, only if you give it, so we can reply
  • the app version, iOS version, whether you have Mango Plus, and which apps you've connected (Apple Health, Apple Watch, Weather, intervals.icu)

It doesn't send your name, location, workouts, health data, plan or any device identifier. Your internet address is used briefly to stop abuse, then discarded, and is never stored.

Feedback is stored by Cloudflare (our hosting provider) in the EU and deleted after two years. If you leave an email address, we reply using Resend (our email provider). We use feedback only to reply to you and to improve the app, never for advertising, and we don't share it with anyone else. To have your feedback deleted sooner, email us.

If you'd rather, "Prefer email? Write to us" opens an email you send yourself.

Deleting your data

You can delete events and change your details in the app. Disconnecting intervals.icu removes its key from your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Workouts already added to your intervals.icu calendar stay there until you delete them in intervals.icu. To delete feedback you've sent, email us and we'll remove it.

Health and safety

Mango's plans are general training guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new programme, and stop if you feel unwell.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone apart from feedback they choose to send.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
