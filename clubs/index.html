Mango Pro

3 months of Mango Plus, free for your club

Mango Pro builds your training plan around your race and adjusts it every morning from how recovered you are. Your club has a code that gives every member three months of Mango Plus free.

How to claim it

  1. Download Mango Pro on your iPhone and build your plan. The plan itself is free.
  2. Tap the claim link your club shared, or open the App Store, tap your account picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code, and enter your club's code.
  3. Plus switches on straight away.

After the 3 free months, Mango Plus is £7.99 a month unless you cancel. Mango reminds you 2 days before, and you can cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Codes are for people who aren't already subscribed.

What Plus adds

Morning readiness. Mango compares your resting heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and sleep with your usual, and suggests an easier day when you need one.

Indoor rides to MyWhoosh. Winter turbo sessions go to your free intervals.icu calendar, ready in MyWhoosh, and the rides you finish count toward your plan.

On your wrist. Sessions go to the Workout app on your Apple Watch with your zones and targets.

Your fitness chart, with where it's heading if you follow the plan.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plan and Apple Health data stay on your iPhone.
